Put it on your home screen

Pocket Irish Dance installs straight from your browser, so there's no app store needed. It takes about 20 seconds, and the app then opens like any other.

  1. Open pocketirishdance.com in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (or at the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. You may need to tap More first.
  4. Tap Add. The shamrock icon appears on your home screen.

You're in a different browser. In Chrome on iPhone, tap the Share button next to the address bar, then Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, open this page in Safari.

  1. Open pocketirishdance.com in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the menu (the three dots ⋮ at the top right).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install. The shamrock icon appears with your other apps.

Pocket Irish Dance is made for your phone, so it's with you at the feis. Open pocketirishdance.com on your phone and follow the steps for iPhone or Android.
