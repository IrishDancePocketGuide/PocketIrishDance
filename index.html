Say it like you've been doing this for years

Hundreds of Irish dance words, explained simply. Tap the button to hear how each one is said.

Feis
“fesh”

A local Irish dance competition, usually one day.

Feiseanna
“FESH-uh-nuh”

More than one feis. Your weekends, from now on.

Oireachtas
“oh-ROCK-tus”

The regional championship, held once a year.

Ghillies
“GILL-eez”

Soft, laced shoes worn by girls and women.

Put it on your home screen

Pocket Irish Dance installs straight from your browser, with no app store needed. Then it opens like any other app, even offline.

  1. Open pocketirishdance.com in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (at the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. You may need to tap More first.
  4. Tap Add. The shamrock icon appears on your home screen.

You're in a different browser. In Chrome on iPhone, tap the Share button next to the address bar, then Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, open this page in Safari.

  1. Open pocketirishdance.com in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the menu (the three dots ⋮ at the top right).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install. The shamrock icon appears with your other apps.
