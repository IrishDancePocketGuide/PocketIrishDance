What TCRG, ADCRG and the other letters mean

TCRG means a certified teacher. Mostly CLRG, but CRDM uses the same title for its own teachers. ADCRG means a certified adjudicator (judge). Mostly CLRG, but CRDM uses the same title for its own judges.

The letters

CLRG (An Coimisiún)CLRG only

Say “un KUM-ish-oon”. An Coimisiún le Rincí Gaelacha, the largest Irish dance organization, based in Dublin. Sets the rules your dancer competes under.

TCRGCLRG onlyCRDM only

A certified teacher. Mostly CLRG, but CRDM uses the same title for its own teachers. Your dancer competes as their TCRG's pupil. Families register for regular feiseanna themselves, but the TCRG registers dancers for majors like the Oireachtas, Nationals and Worlds.

ADCRGCLRG onlyCRDM only

A certified adjudicator (judge). Mostly CLRG, but CRDM uses the same title for its own judges. The people scoring your dancer.

SDCRGCLRG only

A certified CLRG examiner, who can run grade exams. Mostly relevant to teachers.

ADCRG-BGCLRG only

A CLRG teacher and judge who can also examine the first 10 grade exams, but isn't a full examiner (SDCRG) Mostly relevant if your school does grade exams.

IDTANACLRG only

Irish Dance Teachers' Association of North America, the body for CLRG teachers in North America. Its regions run the North American Oireachtas events.

TMRFCLRG onlyWIDA onlyCRDM only

A teacher qualification. In CLRG it's a céilí teacher's certificate, for teaching team dances only. WIDA and CRDM use the title for their own teachers too. Teachers with only TMRF can still compete themselves.

ODCRNCRN only

A fully qualified CRN teacher (Oide Damhsa diploma) The CRN version of a teacher's title.

TMCRNCRN only

A CRN teacher who has passed Part 1 of the ODCRN teacher diploma. A certified, part-qualified CRN teacher.

ADCRNCRN only

A CRN adjudicator (judge) The people scoring your dancer at CRN feiseanna.

Adjudicator

A judge. In grades one judge places each dance. Championships use a panel: usually 3, 5 at major championships, and 6 at the All-Irelands with 5 counting.

Feis committee

The volunteers running a particular feis. Contact them about entries, schedules and results.
