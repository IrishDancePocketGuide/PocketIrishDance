CRN rules in plain words

Short answers to the questions parents ask most about Cumann Rince Náisiúnta (CRN) rules.

CRN's Rules of Competition aren't published online. CRN asks people to contact them for a copy, so ask your teacher, who has them.

Entering competitions

Where can I read CRN's rules?

CRN's Rules of Competition aren't on its website. CRN says to contact them for a copy, and your teacher will have them. Every CRN feis is run under those rules and CRN's Code of Ethics.

Can dancers from other organizations dance at CRN feiseanna?

CRN calls itself an open platform and says its feiseanna welcome dancers from all over the world. Check the feis syllabus and with your teacher before entering.
