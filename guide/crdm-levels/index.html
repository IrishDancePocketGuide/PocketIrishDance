CRDM Irish dance levels, explained

The CRDM level ladder from Beginner A to Open Championship: what each level dances, how a dancer moves up, and what it means for parents.

  • Beginner A
  • Beginner B
  • Intermediate
  • Open
  • Preliminary Championship
  • Open Championship

1Beginner A

What they dance
Solo dances, plus the A Award (for dancers with 3 or more dances in Beginner A)
Moving up
Win 1st place in a dance with 6 or more dancers, then move up in that dance. With fewer than 6, a dancer can win that dance 3 times before moving up.
For parents
No solo dress: a class costume, or a skirt or trousers and top. Beginner A dancers may dance again if they stop or miss the start.

2Beginner B

What they dance
Solo dances, plus the B Award (for dancers with 2 or more dances in Beginner B)
Moving up
Win 1st place in a dance with 6 or more dancers, or win it 3 times with fewer.
For parents
Still a class costume, or a skirt or trousers and top.

3Intermediate

What they dance
Solo dances, plus the Intermediate Award.
Moving up
Win 1st place in a dance with 6 or more dancers, or win it 3 times with fewer.
For parents
Winning a Preliminary or Premiership Championship ends the Intermediate Award for your dancer.

4Open

What they dance
The top grade for solo dances.
Moving up
The top solo grade.
For parents
A dancer can be Open in some dances and lower in others.

5Preliminary Championship

And the Premiership Championship, from age 9

What they dance
Two rounds in front of 3 judges, heavy round first. Ages 7 and 8: reel and light or slip jig. Age 9 and up: reel and treble jig. The Premiership (age 9 and up) is slip jig and a traditional set (boys dance a reel)
Moving up
Winning moves those 2 dances to Open. After 3 wins a dancer can't enter it again.
For parents
For dancers aged 7 and up who haven't won an Open Championship with a modern set under 3 or more judges.

6Open Championship

What they dance
Age 9 and up: at least 2 rounds in front of 3 or more judges, one of them a modern set dance. Under 9s have their own Open cups and awards.
Moving up
The top level. Winning one moves all of a dancer's dances to Open.
For parents
Qualifying for the All Irelands, British Nationals and Worlds runs through the regional championships.
