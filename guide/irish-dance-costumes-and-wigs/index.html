Irish dance costumes, solo dresses and wigs

Most dancers start in the school costume, in the school's colors. A solo dress, wig and tan usually come later, as dancers move up, and the rules on when they're allowed depend on your organization, region and level. Talk to the teacher before ordering anything.

What dancers wear

School costume (class costume, school dress)

The dress or outfit in your school's colors. Usually worn until a dancer moves into a solo dress.

Solo dress

A dancer's own competition dress. One of the biggest costs; rules govern when it's allowed.

Wig

A curled wig worn in competition, usually by girls. Often from the middle levels; check age and level rules.

Headband (tiara, crown)

A decorated band that holds a wig in place and matches the dress. Often bought with a solo dress or wig.

Tan

Spray or bottled tan for legs. Rules on makeup and tan vary by age and level.

Blackout costume (blackout)

A plain all-black outfit worn instead of a costume, with simpler hair and make-up. A cheaper option, but whether it's allowed depends on your organization and the dancer's level.

Waistcoat (vest, cummerbund)

Boys' costume: usually trousers, a shirt and tie, and a vest or waistcoat, sometimes with a sash (cummerbund) in the school colors. Boys' costumes cost much less than dresses, but still need to fit well.

Kick pants (bloomers, dance briefs)

Shorts worn under a dance dress or skirt. Often need to match the dress or be a set color.

Before you buy a solo dress

Before you buy

  • Check the rules for when a solo dress, wig and makeup are allowed
    It depends on your organization, region and level. Some beginner levels don't allow them at all.
  • Talk to the teacher before ordering anything
  • Set a budget, and decide new or second-hand
    Second-hand dresses are very common and keep their value. Many sell again later.
  • For a new made-to-order dress, allow several months
  • Try second-hand dresses on in person, or get a return window if buying online

When it arrives

  • Check the fit with the teacher, and ask about room to grow
  • Check skirt length so it can't catch on kicks
  • Store it hung up in a breathable garment bag, away from sunlight
  • Buy a wig that matches your dancer's hair color, and learn to pin it
  • If tan is used, do a test run well before a feis

Quick answers

When can my dancer wear a solo dress?

It depends on your organization, region and level. Some beginner levels don't allow them at all.

Do Irish dancers have to wear wigs?

A curled wig worn in competition, usually by girls. Often from the middle levels; check age and level rules.
