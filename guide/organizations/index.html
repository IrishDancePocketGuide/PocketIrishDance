Irish dance organizations, explained

Irish dance schools each belong to an organization, and each organization has its own teachers, levels, rules and championships. Your teacher can tell you which one your school is in.

CLRG An Coimisiún le Rincí Gaelacha

(say “un KUM-ish-oon le RIN-kee GAY-luh-kuh”)

The largest Irish dance organization. If your school's teacher has the letters TCRG after their name, you dance under CLRG.

Where
Worldwide. Based in Dublin, with regions across Ireland, Great Britain, North America, Australia, mainland Europe and beyond.
Teachers
TCRG (teacher), ADCRG (adjudicator, or judge)
Major events
Regional Oireachtas, Nationals, All-Irelands, World Championships (Oireachtas Rince na Cruinne)

CLRG levels · CLRG rules · Official website

IDTANA Irish Dance Teachers' Association of North America

The association of CLRG teachers in North America. It looks after the feis rules there, including the level names and how dancers move up, and its regions run the regional Oireachtas championships each year.

Where
United States, Canada and Mexico.
Part of
CLRG
Teachers
Members are CLRG-qualified teachers
Major events
Regional Oireachtas events and the feis rules in North America

An Comhdháil An Comhdháil Múinteoirí Rince Gaelacha

(say “un KOH-loy”)

The second-largest Irish dance organization, with its own teachers, grades and championships. Its grades use Irish names: Bun Grád (Beginners A, B and C), Meán Grád (Pre-Open) and Ard Grád (Open).

Where
Mainly Ireland, with schools in Great Britain and elsewhere.
Teachers
Its own registered teachers, organised in local branches
Major events
Provincial Championships / Qualifiers and the World Championships

An Comhdháil levels · An Comhdháil rules · Official website

WIDA World Irish Dance Association

An independent organization whose feiseanna are open to dancers from any organization, and to independent dancers with no school. All WIDA feiseanna are run the same way.

Where
Mainly mainland Europe, with members in other countries.
Teachers
TCRG and TMRF teacher exams
Major events
National Oireachtasaí, the International and World Championships

WIDA levels · WIDA rules · Official website

CRN Cumann Rince Náisiúnta

(say “KUM-un RIN-kuh NAW-shoon-tuh”)

Founded in Ireland in 1982. CRN describes itself as an open platform: its feiseanna welcome dancers from all over the world. Its teachers are encouraged to choreograph new work while keeping the traditions of Irish dance.

Where
Ireland, the UK, mainland Europe (including the Netherlands and Belgium), the USA, Canada and Australia.
Teachers
TMCRN (part-qualified teacher), ODCRN (fully qualified teacher), ADCRN (adjudicator, or judge)
Major events
Irish Open Championships, North American Open Championships and the World Open Championships (Craobhacha Oscailte na Cruinne). Regional championships are the qualifiers.

CRN rules · Official website · Official school list

CRDM Cumann Rince Dea Mheasa

(say “KUM-un RIN-kuh dyah VASS-uh”)

Founded in 2002 by 8 teachers. All CRDM feiseanna run under CRDM's own rules, and your teacher enters your dancer (CRDM doesn't take entries from parents). Good to know: CRDM teachers also use the titles TCRG and ADCRG, so those letters alone don't tell you a school is CLRG.

Where
Ireland, England, Scotland and the Isle of Man, with some feiseanna in the USA.
Teachers
TMRF and TCRG (teachers) and ADCRG (adjudicator, or judge), from CRDM's own exams
Major events
World Championships, All Ireland Championships, British Nationals and American Nationals
