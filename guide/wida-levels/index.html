WIDA Irish dance levels, explained

The WIDA level ladder from Beginner to Open Championship: what each level dances, how a dancer moves up, and what it means for parents.

  • Beginner
  • Primary
  • Intermediate
  • Open
  • Preliminary Championship
  • Open Championship

1Beginner

The very youngest can start with the Jump 2-3 competition

What they dance
Solo dances at beginner speed, plus the Beginner Premiership (reel and light jig)
Moving up
Win 1st place in a dance with 5 or more dancers, then move up in that dance.
For parents
Every beginner reel dancer is placed and gets a medal.

2Primary

What they dance
Solo dances, plus the Primary Premiership (reel and light jig)
Moving up
Win 1st place in a dance with 5 or more dancers.
For parents
Treble jig and hornpipe can be danced at beginner or open speed until the end of 2026; open speed only from 2027.

3Intermediate

What they dance
Solo dances, plus the Intermediate Premiership (reel and treble jig; under-12s may dance light jig instead until the end of 2026)
Moving up
Win 1st place in a dance with 5 or more dancers. After winning 4 Intermediate dances (2 of them hard shoe), move on to Preliminary Championship.
For parents
The step before championships.

4Open

What they dance
The top grade for solo dances.
Moving up
The top solo grade.
For parents
A dancer can be Open in some dances and lower in others.

5Preliminary Championship

What they dance
Two rounds: reel or slip jig, then treble jig or hornpipe. Only at local feiseanna.
Moving up
Win 3 Preliminary Championships with at least 5 dancers (at least 3 in America and Australasia)
For parents
Every competitor is placed, and 1st to 5th get a trophy.

6Open Championship

What they dance
Two rounds, and a recall to dance a Modern Set if the judges choose. The path to Oireachtas, Internationals and Worlds.
Moving up
The top level. Winning one moves all of a dancer's solo dances up to Open.
For parents
Qualifying for Worlds starts at your National Oireachtas the year before.
Older dancers
WIDA has no separate adult championship, but its age groups go up to Senior, and there is an Over 45 Open Championship (from 2027: hornpipe, reel, Jockey to the Fair and a modern jig set). Dancers over 25 dance 8 fewer bars in each dance.
