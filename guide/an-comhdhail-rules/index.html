An Comhdháil rules in plain words

Short answers to the questions parents ask most about An Comhdháil Múinteoirí Rince Gaelacha (An Comhdháil) rules. Taken from the An Comhdháil public rulebook.

  • Entering competitions
  • At the feis
  • Moving up
  • Championships
  • Shoes and costume
  • Changing schools
  • Penalties

Entering competitions

Which age group does my dancer dance in?

Their age on 1 January decides it for the whole year. Every dancer's birth certificate must be registered with the local branch secretary.

Rulebook section 4.1, 4.3

Can my dancer enter other organizations' competitions?

Dancers who live outside Ireland and the UK may enter open platform competitions that aren't registered with An Comhdháil.

Rulebook section 4.2

At the feis

Can I film or photograph my dancer?

No. Any unofficial photos or video of a dancer while they're competing are forbidden, on phones, tablets or cameras. Live coverage is allowed at the World Championships.

Rulebook section 10.1

What if my dancer falls or a lace comes undone?

If a dance is interrupted by the dancer's own mishap (a lace, a fall, a wig), they can dance again once. A second mishap of the same kind means disqualification. Dancers who stop because someone else fell aren't penalised.

Rulebook section 5.10, 5.12

Are any moves banned?

Dancing en pointe is banned up to and including Under 14 (it's allowed from the September before Under 15), and horizontal split jumps are banned for safety. Either one means disqualification.

Rulebook section 5.9

Moving up

When does my dancer move up from Beginners A, B or C?

When they win 1st place in a dance with at least 10 dancers, they move up in that dance. With fewer dancers, the judge decides whether to give a 1st place or just an award, based on the standard.

Rulebook section 5.1–5.3, 5.7

How does Pre-Open work?

Pre-Open (Meán Grád) is danced in age groups, and a dancer may dance one year above their age. Winning 1st with at least 5 dancers moves them up in that dance. Win in an older age group and you can dance it again the next year.

Rulebook section 5.4, 5.7

My dancer won, but we already paid for the next feis. Can they still dance that grade?

No. Once a dancer wins 1st place in a dance, they can't dance it in that grade again, even if entries are already paid.

Rulebook section 4.5

What are cup competitions?

Special competitions at beginner and Pre-Open level. Your dancer must be in that grade for the dance and have danced a matching solo there. Only one cup per grade per feis, and the winner moves up in that dance.

Rulebook section 4.11–4.12

Championships

When can my dancer enter a Championship?

After dancing at least one Open solo (not the treble reel) at that feis, plus both solo dances in the Championship. Open Championships start at Under 10 (Under 9 at Provincials). A Championship is a light and a heavy dance.

Rulebook section 4.6–4.8

What happens when my dancer wins a Championship?

With 1 judge, they become Open in the dances they danced for that year. With 3 judges, they're Open in every dance for life, and can't go back to Beginners or Pre-Open unless they win no Open medal for two years and apply to be re-graded.

Rulebook section 4.6, 4.9

What is a Non-Championship Holder (NCH) competition?

A competition for dancers who haven't won a Championship, with a light and a heavy dance and one judge. The winner becomes Open in those dances and can't enter NCH again.

Rulebook section 5.8

How does my dancer qualify for the World Championships?

By dancing at a Provincial Championship or Qualifier (only one per season). If something outside your control stops them, tell your branch in writing within 10 days. Schools must also enter figure championships at Provincials to take part in team events at Worlds.

Rulebook section 7.1, 8.1, 8.2

Shoes and costume

When can my dancer wear a solo costume?

Not in Beginners A, B or C. Up to and including Under 9, a class figure costume is the only class costume allowed.

Rulebook section 2.1–2.2

When can my dancer wear make-up?

No face make-up up to and including age 12, and in team competitions for Under 12 and below. No glitter on the face at any age.

Rulebook section 2.9–2.11

What are the other costume and shoe rules?

Dance shoes must be black (white laces are fine). No arm aids. No names or initials on solo costumes, and no designs showing past wins (such as globes). Wear the number in the centre of the costume. Boys over 11 wear soft shoes for the reel.

Rulebook section 2.4–2.8, 4.6

Changing schools

What happens if we change schools?

Dancers moving into or within An Comhdháil have a 3-month restyling period before they can compete, starting when the new teacher's branch receives the form. For brand-new beginners it only applies after a month of classes, and not if the old class closed. After restyling, they must dance a Provincial Championship before the World Championships.

Rulebook section 3.1–3.9

Penalties

What if my dancer is entered in the wrong grade or age?

It's taken seriously: dancing in the wrong grade means a 3-month suspension, and in the wrong age group a 12-month suspension. Double-check entries.

Rulebook section 9.1–9.2
