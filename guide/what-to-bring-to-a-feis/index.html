What to bring to a feis

The packing list. Pack the night before and keep this list in the bag. The short version: both pairs of shoes, socks and sock glue, the costume on a hanger, the competitor number and pins, hair things, a small fix-it kit, plenty of food and water, and a charged phone.

Shoes and socks

  • Soft shoes
  • Hard shoes (if your dancer dances them)
  • Poodle socks or tights, plus a spare pair
    Socks get dirty and laddered tights happen. Keep a clean pair in a zip bag.
  • Sock glue, in a sealed bag so it can't leak
  • Spare laces, plus a spare buckle or strap for hard shoes
  • Electrical tape and sports tape
    Used to secure laces and straps, and to fix almost anything in a hurry.
  • Something for slippery stages (ask the teacher what's allowed)
  • A black marker to touch up scuffs on hard shoes
  • Comfortable shoes or slides for walking around between dances

Costume and number

  • Costume or school outfit, on a hanger in a garment bag
  • Dance briefs, bloomers or shorts, and a spare
  • A cover-up or zip jacket to wear over the costume
  • Safety pins or number clips for the competitor number
  • The competitor number, if you got it ahead of time
  • Headpiece, tiara or brooch, if your dancer wears one

Hair, wig and makeup

  • Wig or bun wig, in its box or a bag so curls don't crush
  • Lots of hair pins and wig grips
  • Hair ties and a headband for the wig
  • Hairspray and a brush
  • Makeup and tan, only if allowed for your dancer's age and level
    Rules on makeup and tan depend on the organization and the dancer's age. Check the Rules section.
  • Baby wipes and makeup wipes

Fix-it kit

  • Needle, thread and small scissors
  • Spare crystals and glue, sealed in their own bag
  • A stain pen
  • Zip-top bags to keep everything sorted and dry

First aid and comfort

  • Plasters (band-aids) and blister pads
  • Pain relief and any regular medicine your dancer needs
  • An instant ice pack
  • Deodorant (the thing everyone forgets)
  • Tissues and hand sanitizer
  • A warm layer and a blanket; halls can be cold
  • A book, cards or something quiet to pass the waiting time

Food and drink

  • Plenty of water, plus a sports drink for long days
  • Snacks that won't stain or crumble on the costume
    Think bananas, crackers, cereal bars, pretzels. Skip anything red, orange or sticky near the costume.
  • A packed lunch; venue food lines can be long
  • Any allergy needs covered, since feis food is limited
  • Coffee or snacks for you too

Phone and paperwork

  • Stage schedule screenshot and entry confirmation
  • A charged phone and a power bank
  • Some cash for parking, programs, raffles or vendors
  • A pen and the teacher's phone number

Quick answers

What should be in a feis bag?

Shoes and socks (with spares and sock glue), the costume and competitor number, hair and wig supplies, a fix-it kit, first aid, food and drink, and your phone and paperwork. Think bananas, crackers, cereal bars, pretzels. Skip anything red, orange or sticky near the costume.

Why bring tape to a feis?

Used to secure laces and straps, and to fix almost anything in a hurry.
