CRDM rules in plain words

Short answers to the questions parents ask most about Cumann Rince Dea Mheasa (CRDM) rules. Taken from the CRDM competition rules.

Entering competitions

How do I enter a CRDM feis?

Through your teacher. CRDM only takes entries from its registered teachers (and teachers on its approved visitors list), not from parents. All fees are paid in advance; you can't pay on the day. Questions about entries also go through your teacher.

Rulebook section CRDM020 1.3, CRDM094

What if a feis is cancelled or moved?

If a feis is cancelled and rescheduled to a date that doesn't suit your child, you're entitled to a full refund of the fee. If a one-day feis becomes two days, dancers whose day changed get a refund. CRDM doesn't cover travel or hotel costs.

Rulebook section CRDM094

Which age group does my dancer dance in?

Their age on the day of the feis decides it.

Rulebook section CRDM019

At the feis

Can I film or photograph my dancer?

No. Cameras and phones aren't allowed during competitions, to protect children. Photos are only allowed during the prize giving.

Rulebook section CRDM020 1.4, CRDM094

When should my dancer be ready?

In costume and ready to dance 30 minutes before their competition is scheduled. The hall must be silent while anyone is dancing, so don't move about during a competition.

Rulebook section CRDM094

What behaviour is expected from families?

No smoking, vaping or alcohol. Children shouldn't run around or shout, and parents are responsible for them. Dancers applaud every competitor when results are called, and there's no booing or shouting at the stage. Bullying leads to a warning and removal from the venue, and repeated bullying to a ban from CRDM events. CRDM also forbids posting on the Voy forum.

Rulebook section CRDM020 1.5–2.3

What if my dancer stops or misses the start?

Only Beginner A dancers are allowed to dance again. Everyone else can't redance.

Rulebook section CRDM094

Moving up

When does my dancer move up a grade?

When they win 1st place in a dance with 6 or more dancers, they move up in that dance. If there were fewer than 6, they can win that dance in that grade 3 times before they must move up. It's one dance at a time.

Rulebook section CRDM094

How many competitions can my dancer enter?

Solos in up to 3 different grades, and at most 3 awards or championships at one feis (not two of the same grade). To enter an award or 1 Adjudicator Championship, your dancer must also dance at least 2 solo dances that day. Boys may enter only 1 championship.

Rulebook section CRDM094, CRDM019

Awards and championships

What are the A, B and Intermediate Awards?

Competitions in front of 1 judge. The A Award is for dancers with 3 or more solo dances in Beginner A, the B Award for 2 or more in Beginner B, and the Intermediate Award for dancers who haven't won it or any championship. Winning one means moving up to the next Award and moving that dance up too. With fewer than 6 dancers, a winner can enter that Award again at the next championship feis.

Rulebook section CRDM019

Who can dance the Preliminary or Premiership Championship?

Preliminary: dancers aged 7 and up who haven't won an Open Championship with a modern set under 3 or more judges. Premiership: the same, from age 9. Both are danced 2 at a time in front of 3 judges, heavy round first. Winning moves those 2 dances to Open, and after 3 wins (Preliminary and Premiership wins added together) a dancer can't enter again.

Rulebook section CRDM019, CRDM023

When can my dancer dance an Open Championship?

When they're no longer eligible for the A or B Award. From age 9 it's at least 2 rounds in front of 3 or more judges, one of them a modern set dance. Winning one moves all of a dancer's dances to Open, and they can't enter the Intermediate Award, Preliminary or Premiership again. Under 9s have their own Open cups and awards.

Rulebook section CRDM019, CRDM094

How does my dancer qualify for the All Irelands, British Nationals or Worlds?

All Irelands and British Nationals: dance at 4 or more CRDM feiseanna (1 for dancers in remote places), and dance a reel for it to count. Worlds: your dancer can't be eligible for the A or B Award, and must dance 2 Open Championships at regional championships plus a 1 Adjudicator Championship at a regional grade feis (one qualifying championship for remote dancers).

Rulebook section CRDM023 8–10

Shoes and costume

What shoes are allowed?

Black leather or patent soft shoes for light dances and black hard shoes for heavy dances. No coloured or sparkly shoes, even for prize giving. No buckles or elastic on soft shoes, and buckles on hard shoes can be at most 2 inches by 1 inch.

Rulebook section CRDM032

What are the costume rules?

Beginner A and B dancers can't wear a solo dress: a class costume, or a skirt or trousers and top with sleeves, also during results. Shoulders must be covered, skirts no shorter than mid-thigh at the back, and costumes no shorter than your dancer's hand held at their side. White bubble socks or black tights (at least 70 denier). No feathers. Change in the changing rooms.

Rulebook section CRDM033

Changing schools

What happens if we change schools?

A dancer can't be judged by their old teacher for 2 years. If they move to a school in another organization, any perpetual trophies must go back to the feis organiser straight away.
